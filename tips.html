💙 Wellness Tips

Sleep Well

Sleep 7–8 hours daily to improve concentration, memory, and mental health.

Stay Hydrated

Drink at least 2–3 liters of water every day to stay active and healthy.

Eat Healthy

Include fruits, vegetables, proteins, and whole grains in your daily diet.

Study Smart

Study in 25-minute sessions with short breaks using the Pomodoro Technique.

Meditate

Practice meditation or deep breathing for 10 minutes every day to reduce stress.

Exercise

Walk, jog, or exercise for at least 30 minutes daily to improve physical and mental fitness.
